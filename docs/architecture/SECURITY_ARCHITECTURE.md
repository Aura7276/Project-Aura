Creator private growth data, drafts, earnings and AI history remain owner-scoped. Brands access only authorized professional projections or shared collaboration objects. Brand strategy and verification evidence require separate grants. Agency labels confer no client rights. Platform administrators require separate provisioning, MFA and purpose-scoped audited operations. Recovery drills, legal retention and real provider tests remain release gates.

M03 Creator onboarding data (goals, challenges, targets, resources, rates, social observations) is owner-scoped. The workspace is resolved server-side and re-checked by every database function for session, owner membership, Creator workspace kind and status. Tables have no grants, profile visibility is constrained to private, screenshot upload is disabled until private scanned storage exists, and user-entered URLs are validated but never fetched. Rate limiting is per instance only. Live Supabase RLS and Storage tests remain release gates.

M04 Creator Studio data (daily actions, observations, ideas, reviews, preferences) is owner-scoped and reachable only through functions that re-authorize the session, Creator workspace and activation. Search runs in the database over the caller's own workspace only, escapes wildcards, stores no queries and shows no counts of unauthorized records. Notifications show only whitelisted real events with no unread state. Authenticated routes are `no-store` with the nonce CSP. Error boundaries show no detail. The framework's redirect payload for an anonymous caller echoes only that caller's own request URL (escaped) and no Creator data. Live Supabase RLS tests remain a release gate.

## M05 security

Restricted reviewers need Platform Admin, a reviewer grant and an MFA session; no self-approval. Evidence is private, validated, unscanned-until-scanner, signed-URL only, and disabled by default. No social credentials are requested. Creator RPCs cannot set `ai_confirmed`. No server-side URL fetching.

## M06 security

Analytics, insights and experiments are readable only by the owning Creator workspace. Audience grants are Brand-only, purpose-bound, revocable and re-checked on read and export. Exports record purpose and time (never content), neutralize spreadsheet formulas and require a live session. Cohort output is suppressed below a floor of 10 and never reveals size or identity. No client function can create AI-proposed tags, competitors or provider data. Insight links are restricted to in-app paths and categories exclude fraud verdicts.

## M09 security

Share and invitation tokens are hashed at rest and shown once. Restricted links require the named workspace's signed-in member. Public projections are allowlisted and rebuilt on every read. Testimonials are attributable, invitation-only, never by the Creator's own workspace, and moderated by an MFA reviewer. No visitor identity is stored. External links are https only and open with `noopener noreferrer`. Kit pages send `no-referrer`.

## M10 security

Publication and invitations require approved verification at every visibility. Restricted and invitation-only campaigns are checked on every read, including direct URLs. Reviewers need MFA, give reasons and cannot review their own workspace. There is no fee field; fee language is held for review and reportable. Reports are not shown to the Brand. Saved-search filters are allowlisted and bounded.

## M11 security

Brands see only what a Creator shared for that application. Creators are addressed by published username, never by workspace id. Invitations, enquiries and Brand proposals need approved verification; Creators can block Brands and report invitations, enquiries and messages to an MFA reviewer. Daily rate limits and idempotent forms stop duplicates and flooding. Agencies are denied until delegation is approved.

## M14 security

Business verification is per workspace and needs a reviewer with MFA who is not a member of the workspace; appeals need a different reviewer; every decision has a reason. Identity documents are never stored in full (last 4 characters only). Legal identity, representatives, budgets, audiences and plans are private; the public profile is an allowlisted projection of a verified, published Brand. A legal identity change pauses outreach until revalidated. Brand writes need an owner or admin with MFA; an agency name grants no access.
