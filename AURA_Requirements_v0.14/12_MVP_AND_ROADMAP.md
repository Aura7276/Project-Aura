# MVP and roadmap — provisional planning only
Approved requirements do not mean approved launch scope. M15–M30 still pending. No development of unapproved modules as if finalized.

| Proposed increment | Modules | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | foundation | audit existing repo, real auth, tenant/RLS, staging, QA |
| 1 | M01–M03 | public site, auth, Creator onboarding |
| 2 | M04–M06 | Creator dashboard, manual social data, basic analytics |
| 3 | M07–M09 | limited AI, content workflow, portfolio/basic kit |
| 4 | M14 | Brand onboarding, verification gates, basic private tools |
| 5 | M10–M13 | shared commercial discovery, applications, agreements, manual compensation — depends on approved M17/M18 Brand workflows |
| 6 | later approved modules | integrate M15–M30 after decision review |

Suggested minimum validation release: real sign-in, Creator onboarding including aspiring path, manual metrics, useful dashboard, idea/calendar/portfolio, verified Brand onboarding and safe discovery. Commercial end-to-end pilot only once Brand campaign creation/selection, moderation, agreements and financial records are approved and tested. Defer sophisticated AI/media, cohort benchmarks, advanced reporting, competitor monitoring, social APIs, automated publishing, payment processing, escrow, financial products and advanced signatures. Final MVP boundaries require founder approval.
