# Consolidated business rules

## M01 — Public website and navigation
Public fields allowlisted: published display name/photo/niche/bio/languages/general location/content and opted-in follower range. No private rates, phone, email, analytics or campaign details. No fake endorsements, counts or opportunities. Referral abuse controls; reward policy M27.

## M02 — Registration and authentication
Account, membership, onboarding, verification and MFA are independent. Workspace authorization checked on every read/write. M14 permits multiple isolated Brand workspaces. Do not grant admin privileges via public sign-up.

## M03 — Creator onboarding
At least one relevant individual account with 100+ followers for standard influencer privileges (M05); never sum followers to qualify. Separate UGC eligibility. Private by default. No fabricated metrics. Proposed segments 0–99/no account, 100–999, 1k–4,999, 5k–9,999, 10k–19,999, 20k+.

## M04 — Creator dashboard
Personalization never grants permissions. Distinguish zero, unavailable, stale and self-reported data. No creator quality score; no fake wallet. Commercial widgets derive from M13.

## M05 — Social media account management
Unique active verified claim enforced atomically. No unauthorized scraping; do not request social passwords. Self-reported metrics not verified by ownership alone. Revoked visibility invalidates discovery, search and cached outputs.

## M06 — Creator analytics and insights
No sum of unique audiences across platforms without deduplication evidence. Missing data not zero. Compare same denominators and measurement windows. Cohorts suppress small samples. Private diagnostics never feed Brand rankings.

## M07 — AI Growth Assistant
Server-mediated tools with scoped authorization, no autonomous commercial commitments, no hidden sharing, no training by default. Treat retrieved data as untrusted; do not expose secrets. Use rationale summaries, not private model reasoning.

## M08 — Content ideation and planning
Production state independent of contract approval. Proposed idea/planned/drafting/production/editing/review/revision/ready/published/analyzed/paused/archived/cancelled. Planned date not actual publication. Calendar edits cannot silently change contractual deadlines.

## M09 — Creator profile and media kit
Separate private, public, discovery and restricted kit projections. Hosted snapshots enforce current privacy/revocation. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled. No fabricated experience/testimonials; rates not contracts.

## M10 — Campaign marketplace
Mandatory eligibility independent of ranking. Conditional requirements checked at correct stage. Public previews limited; private campaign direct URL cannot bypass grant. No fabricated counts. Material changes do not amend accepted agreement. M14 requires business verification for all formal publication/outreach.

## M11 — Applications and invitations
Proposed app states draft/submitted/review/clarification/shortlisted/waitlisted/selected/rejected/withdrawn/expired/cancelled/converted. Negotiation state separate. Atomic active application/capacity checks. Proposal edit resets confirmation. M14 verified outreach; no automatic binding agreement.

## M12 — Collaboration and deliverables
Same-version immutable accepted agreement; external attachment discrepancies resolved before activation. Review exact stage and version. Do not auto-approve by default. Separate operational, deliverable, financial lifecycle. Disputed flags independent. No payment processing, binding arbitration or guaranteed external licence enforcement.

## M13 — Earnings and commercial management
Use exact monetary precision and original currencies. Outstanding = adjusted eligible obligation less allocated receipts and valid credits; refunds/reversals adjust allocations. Overdue/disputed flags independent. Never count invoice as duplicate obligation. Separate barter, contingent commission, reimbursements and cash. No wallet, bank verification claim or payment commission.

## M14 — Brand onboarding
No Creator 100-follower threshold for Brands. Verification per workspace; no public/private outreach bypass. Legal identity changes revalidate. Existing obligations not erased by revocation. Public identity distinct from legal evidence. AI imports are drafts pending confirmation.

## Precedence
Latest explicit approved decision prevails: M04 clarifies M03 aspiring eligibility; M05 individual account threshold; M14 extends verification to all campaign visibility and commercial enquiries, and extends privileged MFA. M12 separates agreement, deliverables and financial settlement. M13 prohibits fund holding and payment-processing commission during manual release.
