## Recovered requirements and established cross-module constraints

- Compare candidates using compatible, authorized metrics, portfolio evidence, deliverables, compensation, rights, availability and relevance.

- Preserve the approved 72-hour selection reservation concept; release expired reservations and revalidate before activation.
