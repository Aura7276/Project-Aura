# Project AURA — product vision
Version 0.14 | 24 November 2026 | requirements baseline

India-first creator growth, marketplace and business management platform, with a complementary Brand marketing workspace and future Agency operations. Creators first: help people grow before they need a campaign; use only permitted professional intelligence to connect them with relevant businesses. Brand value combines creator-led marketing with owned-channel growth. The target segments include aspiring creators, 100–999, 1k–5k, 5k–10k, 10k–20k and 20k+; boundaries proposed as non-overlapping in M03.

Success measures (proposed, not targets): activation, meaningful weekly use, content planning-to-publication completion, evidence-backed growth experiments, eligible applications, completed collaborations, repeat Brand engagement, commercial reconciliation. Validate with real users; do not claim market demand, ROI or financial projections without evidence.

Commercial principles: core Creator tools and eligible applications initially free; Brand registration and basic onboarding free; commercial subscriptions and advanced tools decided M27. Manual settlement, no funds held or processed. Meta integration deferred. No fake production data. Agency, Admin and remaining Brand features are not yet approved beyond cross-module constraints.
